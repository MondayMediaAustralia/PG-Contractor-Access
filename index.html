Scope of Services

The following outlines the scope of services and associated pricing for Positively Green. Pricing is per job as described below.

DescriptionTotal (ex. GST)
Contractor Logins
Contractor logins.

Each contractor gets their own login using their own email address, linked to their Subcontractor Library record and invited from the Staff page. Logins are never shared. There’s no limit on the number of contractors: Pema, Gary, Troy and Jesse are set up first, and PG can add more later through the Staff page with no development.

Instant off switch.

Setting a contractor to Inactive on the Staff page removes their access immediately. PG doesn’t need to ask us to revoke a login.

Data-level access rules for the contractor role.

Contractors can only reach the jobs assigned to them, and only the parts of those jobs they need to do the work. They can’t see client pricing, quotes, invoices, plan values, stock costs, enquiries, client history or anyone else’s jobs. This is enforced in the data, not just hidden on screen.

Contractor mobile app.

A cut-down version of the technician app showing:

  • their own jobs and weekly schedule;
  • site address, access notes and instructions;
  • lawn area, previous notes and photos;
  • the client’s name and phone number.

They complete jobs with photos, notes, product used and the completed works report. It doesn’t include adding extra work on site, spray logs or any office screens.

Scheduling modes.

Each contractor is set to one of three modes on their Staff record, and the office can change it at any time:

  • the office sets the date;
  • the contractor sets it and it goes straight onto the schedule;
  • the contractor proposes a date for the office to approve.

A contractor can decline a job with a reason, which notifies the office and returns the job for reassignment. The client gets the usual booking confirmation in every mode.

Contractor pricing and purchase orders.

Each contractor is set to one of three pricing methods:

  • a rate card per job type, where the rate loads automatically;
  • quoted per job, where the office enters the agreed price;
  • hourly plus parts, where costs start as “to be confirmed” until the invoice arrives.

Assigning a job creates a draft purchase order for the office to check, which is then sent with the job. Contractors see only their own purchase order and rate. Their invoice is recorded against it, using the existing check that flags a difference of more than 10%, and PG marks it paid by hand.

Contractor stock.

Each contractor gets their own stock location. The office confirms what they collect from the depot, product used is recorded on the job, and unused stock is moved back to the warehouse. Contractors never see stock costs.

Client messages.

“On the way” uses the existing button, worded for contractor jobs, with no GPS or arrival time. Clients only ever see Positively Green, never the contractor’s name or contact details.

Notifications.
  • To contractors, by email and SMS: a job is assigned, a date is approved or changed, or a job is cancelled.
  • To the office, through the existing alert settings: a contractor declines a job, sets a date, or proposes one.
Completed works report approval.

A switch on each contractor’s Staff record holds their reports for office approval before they go to the client. It starts on for every contractor, and PG can turn it off themselves.

Testing and go-live.

We build and test against test contractor accounts before the Siege Cyber pen test, so the contractor role is included in that test. Go-live starts with one contractor before the rest.
